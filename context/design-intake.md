# Design Intake

Use this before implementation.

## Required input

- What screen or flow is being changed?
- Who is the user?
- What is the primary action?
- What must not break?
- Is there an existing component library?
- Are there screenshots, Figma links, or approved examples?
- Does the UI need RTL support?

## Output

Write `frontend-artifacts/design-contract.md`.

Do not begin implementation until the contract exists.
